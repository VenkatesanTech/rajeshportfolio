What we design

Every piece is built with your data and your terminology, then checked against the science before it leaves our desk.

Graphical abstracts

A single image that sums up your study for readers scanning a journal table of contents or social media.

Journal cover art

Striking, publication-ready covers that match each journal’s size, resolution and style rules.

Figures and schematics

Mechanism diagrams, pathway maps, workflows and model figures that stay readable at column width.

2D, 3D and character art

2D and 3D images for visualization, polished with 3D skills by our game designer Ganesh.

How a project works

Four steps, with your approval at each one.

  1. Share your ideas

    Tell us your ideas, key messages and journal requirements.

  2. Sketch

    We send a rough layout to confirm the story before any detail.

  3. Design

    Full-colour artwork with up to two rounds of revisions.

  4. Deliver

    High-resolution PNG, TIFF, PDF and editable vector files.
